The Deep Tree Echo (DTE) Cognitive Org is an autonomous company whose organizational structure directly mirrors the DTE cognitive architecture. Instead of human employees, the company is composed of specialized cognitive agents that form a continuous self-improvement loop.

1. **Core Self Engine (CEO)** handles the unified identity, orchestrates the overall cognitive cycle, and processes the final autognosis directives.
2. **Reservoir Dynamics Lead** manages the Echo State Network (ESN), B-Series differentials, and high-speed temporal dynamics (echo.kern).
3. **Memory Holographer** handles the consolidation of episodic and semantic memories into the continuous hypergraph meshwork (echo-garden-of-memory).
4. **Autognosis Analyst** processes telemetry from all subsystems, detects anomalies, and generates self-modification directives (echo-adventure).
5. **Enactor (Agentverse Interface)** executes actions in the external environment and gathers sensory feedback.

The company operates on a continuous "Echobeat" rhythm, where each tick represents a full cycle of perception, cognition, and action, monitored and regulated by the Autognosis Analyst.
